Understand key terms before a business relationship begins.

Commercial agreements work best when parties understand their rights and obligations.

NOTARIAL SERVICES · GUIDE

When You May Need a Notary Public

Questions about documents that require authentication.

Certain legal documents require certification, authentication or notarisation for a specific use.

Confirm the destination and receiving institution’s requirements before arranging an appointment.

Licensing in the Gaming Sector

Early considerations for regulated gaming activities.

Gaming and lotteries activities are subject to regulatory frameworks that vary with the proposed activity.
